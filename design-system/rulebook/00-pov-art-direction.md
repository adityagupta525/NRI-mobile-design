# 00 · POV & Art Direction (the thesis)
**Obsidian-Copper thesis:** a private-bank-grade calm. Wealth you can trust, shown with restraint and warmth. Not a trading app's noise — a quiet, premium, tiranga-warm confidence.
- **Feel:** obsidian depth, brushed-copper metal, cream numerics, subtle inner light. Calm > busy. Trust > flash.
- **Decisions:** metal CTAs (radius 8, not pill). Glass inputs. One focal point per screen. Numbers are heroes. Motion is physical but subtle.
- **Anti-patterns:** gradient soup, neon, sticker emojis, dense dashboards, color-only signals, decorative charts, generic Dribbble gloss.
- **The test:** beside Stripe/Linear/Revolut, does it hold? If it feels safe, make one brave move.
