## Persona 1 — Arjun Mehta · "The Tax-Smart Gulf Saver"

**35, IT project manager, Dubai (7 yrs). Salaried, tax-free UAE income. Sends money
home monthly; wants it working, not idle.** *[A: profile]*

**Goals**
- Put monthly surplus into Indian equity MFs without a tax haircut. **[V:** UAE NRIs
  can legally reach **0% CG** under DTAA**]**
- Do everything from his phone, at night, across a timezone gap. **[A]**

**Anxieties / pains**
- **"Am I overpaying tax I don't owe?"** — he's heard UAE NRIs pay 0% but doesn't
  know the **TRC + Form 10F** mechanics, and the **UAE TRC-only-for-previous-year**
  timing trap. **[V]**
- **TDS shock:** AMC deducts at source before crediting, so his redemption looks
  smaller than expected. **[V]**
- **Onboarding silence:** fears uploading documents into another app that goes quiet
  for weeks. **[V]**

**Current workarounds**
- Asks a CA friend / WhatsApp groups whether DTAA "is worth the paperwork." **[A]**
- Sometimes leaves money in **NRE FDs** (tax-free interest) instead of investing,
  out of tax uncertainty. **[V:** NRE FD interest is tax-free — a real alternative he
  weighs**]** **[A: that this is *his* specific behaviour]**

**Trust triggers**
- A **visible "tax saved via DTAA this FY"** number. **[V:** BRD §3.4.5 + demand signal]**
- **Estimated TDS shown *before* he confirms** a redemption. **[V]**
- Clear **SEBI/ARN** credentials (scam wave awareness). **[V]**

**What winning looks like:** Arjun activates DTAA in-app, sees "₹X saved," and moves
his NRE-FD idle cash into SIPs.

## Persona 2 — Priya Nair · "The Locked-Out Silicon Valley Earner"

**38, senior engineer, San Jose (US citizen / green-card NRI). High income, low free
time, high compliance dread.** *[A: profile]*

**Goals**
- Keep an India allocation (family, familiarity) **without triggering a US tax
  nightmare.** **[A: intent]** **[V: the constraint is real]**

**Anxieties / pains**
- **Rejected or frozen:** many AMCs **reject US/Canada NRIs**, or accept then
  **freeze the account.** **[V]**
- **PFIC terror:** Indian MFs are **PFICs** under US law → punitive excess-distribution
  rules + brutal filing. **[V:** community + BRD §3.5]**
- **Discovers eligibility too late** — after sinking time into KYC. **[V]**

**Current workarounds**
- Avoids Indian MFs entirely; parks money in **US-based India/EM ETFs** or NRE FDs.
  **[A: specific behaviour]** **[V: avoidance driven by PFIC is real]**
- Relies on a **US CPA** who charges heavily for PFIC forms. **[A]**

**Trust triggers**
- **Radical honesty up front:** an **eligibility check by country of residence
  *before* onboarding** that says clearly what she can/can't do. **[V:** the pain is
  "found out too late"**]**
- **PFIC risk disclosure** stated plainly, not buried. **[V:** BRD §3.5 mandates it]**

**What winning looks like:** Priya learns in 60 seconds exactly what a US person can
do here, trusts us *because* we told her the hard truth, and either proceeds
informed or leaves a lead for a future GIFT-City/USD product.

> ⚠️ **Scope flag:** much of Priya's ideal solution (USD/GIFT-City funds) is
> **Phase 2/3**. Phase-1 job = **honest eligibility gate + PFIC disclosure + lead
> capture**, not full US enablement. (scope.md)

## Persona 3 — Sam (Samir) Kapoor · "The Consolidating HNI"

**46, finance director, London (12 yrs, UK tax resident). Larger, scattered India
portfolio; values advice and clean repatriation over DIY tinkering.** *[A: profile]*

**Goals**
- **Consolidate** legacy folios, understand true after-tax returns, and **repatriate**
  to the UK without FEMA missteps. **[A: intent]** **[V: the mechanics are real pains]**

**Anxieties / pains**
- **UK ≠ zero tax:** his gains are **taxable in India (20%/12.5%)**, with **FTC**
  claimable in the UK — no DTAA free lunch, and double-compliance to track. **[V:** BRD
  §3.4.3]**
- **Repatriation maze:** NRO **USD 1M/FY cap**, **15CA/CB** above it; "which account
  funded which investment?" **[V]**
- **Status/limbo & penalties** on unconverted folios. **[V]**
- **Scam-wary with larger sums** — wants provable regulatory standing. **[V]**

**Current workarounds**
- Pays a **CA in India** to handle 15CA/CB and ITR; keeps a spreadsheet across apps.
  **[A: specific behaviour]** **[V: fragmentation + CA reliance are real]**

**Trust triggers**
- **Repatriable-vs-NRO labelling** on every holding + a guided **15CA/CB** path.
  **[V:** scope.md utility + BRD §3.6]**
- **Capital-gains / FTC view** he can hand to his accountant. **[V]**
- **Premium, calm, "private-bank" tone** — not a gamified retail app. **[A: preference]**

**What winning looks like:** Sam sees one consolidated, correctly-labelled portfolio,
exports a clean CG statement, and initiates a compliant repatriation.

## Cross-persona synthesis
- **Tax situation is the real segmentation axis**, not age/income. One app must
  *branch by country of residence* early. **[V]**
- **Shared non-negotiable across all three:** *tell me the truth early* — eligibility,
  TDS, repatriability — **before** I invest effort or money. **[V]**
- **Our differentiator (DTAA/tax intelligence)** maps cleanly onto **Arjun (activate)**
  and **Sam (comprehend/repatriate)**; **Priya** is mostly an **honest-gate + lead**
  story in Phase 1. **[V + scope.md]**
