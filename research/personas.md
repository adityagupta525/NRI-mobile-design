## Persona 1 — Arjun Mehta · "The Tax-Smart Gulf Saver" · UAE · **PRIMARY**

**35, IT project manager, Dubai (7 yrs). Salaried, tax-free UAE income. Sends money home
monthly; wants it working, not idle.** *[A: profile]*

**Goals**
- Put monthly surplus into Indian equity MFs without a tax haircut. **[V:** UAE NRIs can
  legally reach **0% CG** under DTAA**]**
- Do everything from his phone, at night, across a timezone gap. **[A]**

**Anxieties / pains**
- **"Am I overpaying tax I don't owe?"** — heard UAE NRIs pay 0% but doesn't know the
  **TRC + Form 10F** mechanics, and the **UAE TRC-issued-for-previous-year-only** timing
  trap. **[V]**
- **TDS shock:** AMC deducts at source before crediting. **[V]**
- **Onboarding silence:** fears another app that goes quiet for weeks. **[V]**

**Current workarounds**
- Asks a CA friend / WhatsApp groups whether DTAA "is worth the paperwork." **[A]**
- Sometimes leaves money in **NRE FDs** (tax-free interest) out of tax uncertainty.
  **[V:** NRE FD interest is tax-free**]** **[A: that this is *his* behaviour]**

**Trust triggers:** visible **"tax saved via DTAA this FY"** **[V]**; **estimated TDS before
he confirms** a redemption **[V]**; clear **SEBI/ARN** credentials **[V]**.

**What winning looks like:** Arjun activates DTAA in-app, sees "₹X saved," and moves his
NRE-FD idle cash into SIPs.

## Persona 2 — Sam (Samir) Kapoor · "The Singapore Consolidating HNI" · Singapore · **CO-PRIMARY**

**46, finance director, Singapore (10 yrs, SG tax resident). Larger, scattered India
portfolio; values advice and clean repatriation over DIY tinkering.** *[A: profile]*
*(Shifted from UK→Singapore in the Part-C corridor-focus amendment.)*

**Goals**
- **Consolidate** legacy folios, **prove 0% CG** legitimately, and **repatriate** without
  FEMA missteps. **[A: intent]** **[V: mechanics are real pains]**

**Anxieties / pains**
- **"Is my 0% actually safe?"** — SG gains are **0% CG under India–SG DTAA (Art. 13)**, but
  it's **not automatic**: needs **COR + Form 10F + no-PE + ITR disclosure**, obtained
  **before redemption**. **[V]** And the **ITAT position is under appeal** — he wants the
  hedge stated. **[V:** BRD §3.4.2]**
- **Repatriation maze:** NRO **USD 1M/FY cap**, **15CA/CB** above it; "which account funded
  which investment?" **[V]**
- **Status/limbo & penalties** on unconverted folios. **[V]**
- **Scam-wary with larger sums** — wants provable regulatory standing. **[V]**

**SG-specific notes (vs UAE — matters for the DTAA flow):** [V unless marked]
- TRC in Singapore = **Certificate of Residence (COR)**, issued by **IRAS**; tax ID =
  **NRIC**. (UAE issues via MoF and **only for the previous year** — different timing/authority;
  our DTAA flow must handle both.)
- **No capital-gains tax** in Singapore → the DTAA residual mechanism yields **0%**. **[V]**
- Must be **SG tax resident in the year the gain arises** + **no PE in India**. **[V]**
- **Banking habits:** DBS/OCBC/UOB, high digital-banking maturity, high trust in *regulated*
  fintech — premium/calm reads as competence, not fluff. **[A — verify in interviews]**

**Current workarounds:** pays a **CA in India** for 15CA/CB + ITR; spreadsheet across apps.
**[A: behaviour]** **[V: fragmentation + CA reliance are real]**

**Trust triggers:** **repatriable-vs-NRO labelling** + guided **15CA/CB** **[V]**; **DTAA
activation (COR + 10F) with "tax saved"** **[V]**; **capital-gains statement** for his
accountant **[V]**; **premium, calm "private-bank" tone** **[A: preference]**.

**What winning looks like:** Sam consolidates into one correctly-labelled portfolio, activates
DTAA with his COR, exports a clean CG statement, and initiates a compliant repatriation.

**What winning looks like (Phase-1):** Priya learns in ~60 seconds that we can't compliantly
serve US persons *yet*, trusts us **because** we told her the hard truth up front, and leaves
a waitlist lead for a future GIFT-City/USD product.

> ⚠️ **Scope:** Priya's full solution (USD/GIFT-City) is **Phase 2/3**. Phase-1 = **early

## Cross-persona synthesis
- **Corridor/tax situation is the segmentation axis.** The app must **branch by country of
  residence early** (pre-KYC eligibility check). **[V]**
- **Both focus personas (Arjun UAE, Sam SG) are DTAA-0% activators** — so **H1 (pre-TDS
  clarity) + H2 (DTAA activation) are co-spearheads** for the focus corridors. The DTAA flow
  must handle **UAE vs SG TRC/COR differences**. **[V]**
- **Shared non-negotiable:** *tell me the truth early* — eligibility, TDS, repatriability —
  before I invest effort or money. **[V]**
- **Priya (US) is a waitlist edge, not a build target** — design the honest gate well, then
  stop. **[V + §C1]**
