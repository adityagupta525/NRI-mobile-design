## The material system (this is what was missing — non-negotiable now)
Obsidian is **layered, not flat black.** Every screen has depth, one warm light source, and tactile surfaces.

1. **Depth stack:** canvas (`bg/canvas` #080D18) → surface (`bg/surface`) → elevated (`bg/elevated`) using **tonal lift + a whisper of grain**, never pure-black flat fills (rulebook 01: dark elevation = raise surface lightness, not shadow).
2. **Grain / noise:** subtle film-grain on elevated surfaces — Ashish's Reports/Goals/Vault/Calculator tiles are the signature. Emboss feel, not texture-for-texture's-sake. ~3–5% noise overlay.
3. **Glass:** frosted translucent surfaces for **segmented controls, chips, sheets, tooltips** (moodboard: mhmoradii range-toggle, web3 price-pill, Eth tooltip). Recipe = low-opacity fill (`rgba(86,86,86,0.30)`) + backdrop blur + **hairline top highlight** (white ~4%) + `Gradient/copperShimmer` stroke.
4. **Copper metal:** primary CTAs are **brushed metal, radius 8** (never pill) — 5-stop copper gradient + `Effect/metalButton` sheen + inner highlight, ink text `#241C11`. Ashish's "Invest now" / "Discover tools" are the reference.
5. **Warm rim / aurora glow:** ONE copper glow per screen, bleeding from a single edge/corner of the hero element (moodboard Eth-balance corner glow, mhmoradii bottom rim). **Warm copper only — NEVER the moodboard's purple/blue/teal.** Restraint: one glow source, low intensity.
6. **Photographic warmth:** any imagery desaturated into copper/sepia and darkened (Ashish's India-Gate tax banner). Never full-colour stock photos.
7. **Numerals:** cream `#F2EEE3`, Tabular, Indian grouping. Hero numbers may take a subtle **metallic/chrome** treatment sparingly (moodboard Eth "4.2", Ghulam numerals) — one per screen max.

## Layout DNA
One focal point per screen. **Generous negative space** (moodboard img 5/7/16 — near-empty is premium). Chunk information (Miller). De-emphasise with colour, not shrinking. If a screen feels dense/feature-stacked (our Fund Detail v1 failure, INDmoney's failure), **cut** — the comparison table, the extra tiles, the third toggle earn their place or leave.
