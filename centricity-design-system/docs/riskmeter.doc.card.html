RiskMeter

SEBI Riskometer — a regulatory element. Levels and disclaimer are verbatim; only the materials are re-skinned (obsidian track, copper-intensity arc + needle). No green→red rainbow.

All 6 levels

Compliance rules

  • Exactly six levels, fixed order: Low · Low to Moderate · Moderate · Moderately High · High · Very High.
  • Label text and the "principal will be at … risk" line are verbatim — never paraphrased or shortened.
  • Re-skin materials only. Copper-intensity ramp respects the copper-only law; do not introduce green/red risk colours.
  • Always shown on fund detail + confirm-order screens for the fund being purchased.

Do & Don't

✓ Do
Pair the meter with the verbatim disclaimer line and the fund name. Point the needle at the fund's declared level.
✕ Don't
Don't animate the needle for drama, invent a 7th level, recolour arcs green/amber/red, or reword the levels.

Accessibility

  • Level is conveyed by text label, not colour alone (colour-blind safe).
  • Needle + active arc give a redundant visual cue to the label.
  • Expose the level as an accessible name, e.g. aria-label="Riskometer: Very High".
