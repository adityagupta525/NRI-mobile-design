BottomSheet

The mobile replacement for every modal, dropdown and picker. Grab handle, sheet surface, top radius 16, scrim behind.

Usage

Anatomy

  • ① 36×4 grab handle, grey-600, centered, 12px top pad.
  • ② title (Figtree 17/600), 16px below handle.
  • ③ content region — lists, chips, form fields.
  • ④ optional sticky footer CTA, safe-area padded (bottom 34).
  • ⑤ scrim: black 50% behind, tap-to-dismiss.

Do & Don't

✓ Do
Use for bank/fund/folio pickers, filters, confirm-order, exit-load warning. Keep the primary CTA in the thumb zone.
✕ Don't
Never use a centered modal or a hover dropdown on mobile. Don't exceed ~70% viewport height without an internal scroll region.

Spacing redlines

Top radius 16 (--radius-lg) · side padding 20 · handle 36×4 at 12 top · footer top border hairline, bottom padding 12 + safe-area(34).

Accessibility

  • Focus is trapped within the sheet while open; Esc / scrim tap / swipe-down all dismiss.
  • Scrim carries role="presentation"; the sheet is role="dialog" with aria-modal.
  • Grab handle + title give a clear affordance and an accessible name.
  • Footer CTA remains above the home indicator (safe-area inset) so it's always tappable.

Motion & haptics

Enterslide up from 100% + scrim fade, --motion-slow 400ms, --ease-spring.
Exitslide down + scrim fade, ease-accelerate, 250ms.
Dragfollows finger; releases past ~30% → dismiss, else spring back.
Hapticlight on present; selection tick on row choose.
