RatingPrompt is a trigger-aware, dismissible rating card — show only after a positive moment (order placed, goal hit). Star row; route 4-5★ to the store and ≤3★ to in-app feedback. Never blocks.

Motion: rise-in. Haptic: selection tick per star. Voice: `voice.success.rate`.

```jsx
<RatingPrompt onRate={handle} onDismiss={close} />
```
