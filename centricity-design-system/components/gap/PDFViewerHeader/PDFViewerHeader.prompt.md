PDFViewerHeader is the statement-viewer header — close, title, page count, and download + share icon actions. Sits above a rendered statement (capital gains, account statement, ELSS proof).

Motion: none. Haptic: light on actions. Voice: n/a.

```jsx
<PDFViewerHeader title="Capital Gains FY24-25" page={1} pageCount={4} onClose={close} onDownload={dl} onShare={share} />
```
