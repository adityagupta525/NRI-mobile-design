RiskMeter is the SEBI Riskometer — a regulatory element. The 6 levels (Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High, Very High) and the "principal will be at … risk" disclaimer are verbatim and must not be restyled into non-compliant wording. Only the materials are re-skinned: obsidian track, copper-intensity active arc, copper needle (copper-only law — no green/red rainbow).

Motion: none (regulatory clarity). Haptic: none. Voice: fixed regulatory copy.

```jsx
<RiskMeter levelIndex={4} />   {/* High */}
```
